FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible. Weekends and the holiday season book up quickly, but reach out even for last-minute events since I'll always do my best to make it work.

Do you travel?

Yes. Let me know your city or venue in the form and I'll include any travel details in your quote.

What kind of space do you need?

For strolling close-up magic, none at all. I come to your guests. For a stage or parlor show, a small open area where everyone can see is ideal.

Is the show family-friendly?

Always. The humor and material are tailored to your audience, whether it's a kids' birthday or an executive dinner.
