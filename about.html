About me

Hi, I'm Aaron. I make the impossible feel personal.

Aaron Tang · Magician & Entertainer

I'm a magician who believes the best magic doesn't happen on a distant stage. It happens inches from your eyes, in your own hands, with your friends gasping beside you.

What started as a fascination with a single card trick turned into years of practice, study and performing for audiences of every kind. Today I bring a modern, conversational style of magic to weddings, corporate events and private celebrations.

My goal at every event is simple: create genuine moments of wonder that bring people together, spark conversation and leave your guests talking long after the night is over.

What to expect

  • Interactive magic where your guests are part of the show
  • Clean, family-friendly humor that fits any crowd
  • Professional, punctual and easy to work with from start to finish
  • Performances tailored to your timeline, venue and audience

Perfect for

Weddings Corporate events Holiday parties Birthdays Trade shows Fundraisers Restaurants & bars School & campus events
