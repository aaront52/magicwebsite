About

Meet Aaron Tang.

Aaron first started performing magic for his family as a child. Inspired by the joy magic could bring to others, especially those who faced health conditions, he led the Magic & Illusion Student Team throughout his undergraduate career at UCLA.

He founded a magic volunteer program at Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center where undergraduate magicians uplifted the spirits of pediatric patients weekly.

Upon graduating from UCLA, Aaron began attending the UCLA David Geffen School of Medicine. As a medical student, Aaron founded the MagicAid student organization, where he has taught 20+ UCLA medical students how to perform magic routines tailored to pediatric patients. He now leads teams of medical students during bedside magic rounds at Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center.

Aaron specializes in close-up magic while also bringing extensive experience in large-scale stage illusions. His performances combine technical precision, engaging storytelling, and carefully constructed sequences of increasingly impossible reveals. Whether performing inches away from audiences or commanding a stage from afar, Aaron creates experiences that leave lasting impressions long after the final reveal.
